The need to gather together

Base Text

"Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together... but exhorting one another, and so much the more as you see the Day approaching." Hebrews 10:24–25

Hebrews does not present the Christian gathering as a product we consume when it is convenient. It says we should consider one another in order to stir up love and good works.

Gathering has a horizontal direction as well as a vertical one: we need others and we are needed by others.

The early church continued in the apostles' teaching, fellowship, the breaking of bread, and prayers. It was not a weekly audience gathered around a personality.

Of course, illness, persecution, distance, and other circumstances can make gathering impossible. The text should not become a hammer against someone who truly cannot attend.

But it does confront the habit of deliberately living an isolated faith.

If your only question is "What did I receive today?" another question is still missing: "Whom did I encourage?"

Christ is forming a people. Seek a community where the Word is taken seriously and learn to remain, serve, correct, and be corrected within it.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
