Quiet envy

Base Text

"For where envy and self-seeking exist, confusion and every evil thing are there." James 3:16

Envy rarely introduces itself by name. It can disguise itself as criticism: we quickly find flaws in what we secretly desire.

James connects bitter envy with earthly and destructive wisdom. By contrast, the wisdom from above produces peace, mercy, and good fruit.

Envy turns another person's blessing into an accusation against God: "Why him and not me?"

Cain allowed that logic to grow into violence. John the Baptist showed another way when his disciples told him that everyone was going to Jesus: "He must increase, but I must decrease." He did not need to be the center in order to know who he was.

When someone else's good news makes you uncomfortable, do not feed comparisons. Deliberately thank God for the good that person has received. Examine what desire of yours has been exposed and bring it to the Lord.

In the kingdom of God, grace given to someone else does not reduce what is available to you.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
