The truth when lying would be easier

Base Text

"Putting away lying, let each one of you speak truth with his neighbor, for we are members of one another." Ephesians 4:25

Paul grounds truthfulness not only in a rule, but in our relationship: we are members of one another.

Lying damages the body because it manipulates the reality we share. Some lies seem small: exaggerating to look good, hiding a fact, promising something we know we will not fulfill.

But each one trains the heart to use truth as a tool instead of submitting to it.

Jesus is the truth, and the devil is called the father of lies. The contrast is serious.

That does not require us to tell everything we know to anyone who asks; prudence and confidentiality exist. But we must never manufacture falsehood in order to protect our image.

If you lied, correct it as soon as possible. Shame grows with time and invites us to build a second lie to support the first.

Speaking truth can cost you reputation for a moment. Over time it forms a life that does not need to remember which version it told.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
