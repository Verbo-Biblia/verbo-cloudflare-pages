When illness comes

Base Text

"Even though our outward man is perishing, yet the inward man is being renewed day by day." 2 Corinthians 4:16

The Bible never romanticizes the frailty of the body. Paul speaks of an outward person wearing away.

Epaphroditus was sick almost to death; Timothy had ailments; believers prayed for healing. Seeking care, medicine, and rest is not a lack of faith.

At the same time, Christian hope does not consist in making the present body never deteriorate. Paul looks toward resurrection and toward what is eternal.

That changes illness without calling it good.

We can ask for healing freely and give thanks when it comes. We can also remain in Christ when it does not come as we desire.

Illness can reduce what we do without reducing our value before God.

If your body is weak, do not measure your faithfulness only by productivity. Receive help. Pray. Care for what can be cared for.

And remember that the risen Jesus is the promise that the corruption of the body will not have the final word.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
