When you receive criticism

Base Text

"Whoever loves instruction loves knowledge, but he who hates correction is stupid." Proverbs 12:1

Not every criticism is correct, but our first reaction is often to defend ourselves before finding out.

Proverbs presents the ability to receive correction as part of wisdom. David had moments when he listened to rebuke and others when he ignored what was obvious until Nathan confronted him.

A criticism can be poorly expressed and still contain something we need to hear. Humility asks: is there truth here?

That does not mean handing our conscience over to every opinion. Jesus was falsely accused, and Paul rejected unjust accusations. We must discern. But there is a difference between discernment and immediately building a defense.

When someone corrects you, listen before responding. Examine the matter before God. If it is false, answer with truth and gentleness when appropriate. If it is true, be thankful for the opportunity to change.

Being corrected does not reduce your value in Christ. It can become an instrument of his grace.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
