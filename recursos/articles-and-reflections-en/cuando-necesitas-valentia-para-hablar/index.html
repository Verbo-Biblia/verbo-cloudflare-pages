When you need courage to speak

Base Text

"God has not given us a spirit of fear... Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord." 2 Timothy 1:7–8

Timothy needed encouragement to testify in a context where identifying with Paul and with the gospel had a cost.

Christian courage is not a loud personality. It can exist in a timid person who decides not to be ashamed of Christ.

Paul joins power, love, and self-control. It is an important combination: power without love becomes aggression; love without truth can become cowardice; self-control keeps courage from becoming impulsiveness.

When you need to speak about Christ or defend a truth, you do not need to turn the conversation into a fight.

Listen, speak clearly, and acknowledge what you do not know. Your task is to be faithful, not to control the other person's reaction.

The gospel does not need our hostility in order to be powerful. It needs witnesses who are not ashamed of the crucified and risen Lord.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
