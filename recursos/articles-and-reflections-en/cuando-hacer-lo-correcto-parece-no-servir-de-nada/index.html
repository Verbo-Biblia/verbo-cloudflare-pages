When doing the right thing seems useless

Base Text

"And let us not grow weary while doing good, for in due season we shall reap if we do not lose heart." Galatians 6:9

There are seasons when doing the right thing seems to accomplish very little. You forgave, and the other person did not change. You were honest, and someone else, cheating, moved ahead faster. You served, and no one noticed. You prayed, and the situation remained the same.

Then an uncomfortable question appears: what is the point of continuing to do what is right?

Paul is not writing to people who do not know God, but to believers called to live by the Spirit and to sow to the Spirit. Therefore, "not growing weary" is not a formula for obtaining the result we want. It is persevering in a life that corresponds to those who belong to Christ.

Scripture never promises that every right decision will receive an immediate reward. Joseph fled from sin and ended up in prison. The apostles obeyed Christ and encountered opposition. Jesus was perfectly righteous and ended up on a cross.

That destroys a dangerous idea: "if I obey, God must make everything go well for me." That is not obedience; it is negotiation.

Paul speaks of sowing and reaping. A seed is not judged the day after it has been planted. In the same way, we should not measure God's faithfulness only by what we see today. Nor should we turn the harvest into a promise of success, money, or recognition. The hope is deeper: what is done in the Lord is not in vain.

Perhaps no one sees your integrity. God does. Perhaps forgiveness does not immediately restore a relationship. Perhaps telling the truth costs you something. The question is not only what result you obtained, but whom you chose to belong to in the middle of that situation.

Look to Christ. The cross seemed to be the defeat of the righteous one. Yet precisely there God was working salvation, and after the cross came the resurrection.

So when you are tired, you do not need to pretend that you are not. But do not allow weariness to decide who you are going to be. Do good, tell the truth, reject sin, keep your word, and serve even when no one is watching. Not to buy God's favor, but because you belong to Christ.

You may not yet see the harvest. That does not mean the seed was useless.

Prayer

Lord, when I grow weary of doing good, remind me that my faithfulness does not depend on what I can see. Help me obey you without bargaining with you and keep my eyes fixed on Christ. Amen.
