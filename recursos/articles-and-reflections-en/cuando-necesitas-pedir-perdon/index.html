When you need to ask for forgiveness

Base Text

"Go your way. First be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift." Matthew 5:23–24

Jesus places reconciliation in a surprisingly high position. If you remember that your brother has something against you, do not use worship as a hiding place. Go.

Asking forgiveness biblically is more than saying, "I am sorry you felt that way." Name what you did without transferring the blame.

Do not demand that the other person absolve you immediately or use your apology to control their reaction.

When Zacchaeus understood the reach of his conduct, he also spoke of restitution. Some injuries require repairing damage where possible.

That does not mean you can buy forgiveness. It means repentance produces fruit.

Perhaps the other person does not want reconciliation. Romans recognizes that peace depends, as far as possible, on us.

Do your part with humility.

Christ reconciled us to God at a cost we could not pay; those of us who live by that grace should not proudly protect our image when we have done wrong.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
