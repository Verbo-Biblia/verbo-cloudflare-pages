When you compare yourself with others

Base Text

"What is that to you? You follow me." John 21:21–22

Peter wanted to know what would happen to John. Jesus returned him to his own responsibility: follow me. Comparison makes us live preoccupied with the story God is writing with someone else. We can turn gifts, ministry, family, money, or spiritual progress into a competition Christ never established.

Paul reminds us that there are different gifts, but the same Lord. The eye does not need to become a hand in order to have value. Your calling does not gain dignity because it is more visible than someone else's. The decisive question is not whether you are moving at another person's pace, but whether you are being faithful to Christ with what you have received.

Also avoid the opposite extreme: "I am different" does not justify laziness or disobedience. Peter still had to follow.

Today perhaps you need to stop looking sideways. Give thanks for what God is doing in others without turning it into the measure of your own life. Receive correction when needed, learn from good examples, and hear again the Lord's simple voice: you, follow me.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
