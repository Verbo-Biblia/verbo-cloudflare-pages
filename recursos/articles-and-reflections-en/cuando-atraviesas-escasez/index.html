When you go through scarcity

Base Text

"I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound... I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." Philippians 4:12–13

"I can do all things through Christ" is often associated with great achievements. In context, Paul is talking about learning to live with abundance and with hunger.

The strength of Christ is not a guarantee of conquering any goal we imagine. It is the ability to remain faithful in changing circumstances.

Paul thanked the Philippians for their financial help; he did not spiritualize his need. The church had participated in meeting it. So trusting God does not exclude asking for help, working, or receiving generosity.

But Paul had learned something circumstances could not give him: contentment. Scarcity was not good in itself, and abundance was not his savior. Christ remained sufficient in both.

If something is lacking today, present your need truthfully. Do what you responsibly can, and allow others to help you. But do not conclude that your life is worth less because you possess less.

The strength promised here is not for pretending invulnerability, but for walking through need without abandoning Christ.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
