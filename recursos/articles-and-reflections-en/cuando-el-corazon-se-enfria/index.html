When the heart grows cold

Base Text

"You have left your first love. Remember... repent and do the first works." Revelation 2:4–5

The church in Ephesus had perseverance and doctrinal discernment, but Christ pointed out a serious loss: it had left its first love.

This reminds us that correct activity and affection toward God are not identical.

We can defend truth, work, and resist error while the heart becomes mechanical.

Jesus does not tell them to chase a spontaneous emotion. He gives them actions: remember, repent, and return to the first works.

When your relationship with God feels cold, you do not need to manufacture feelings. Remember where Christ brought you from. Examine what displaced your love.

Return to abandoned obediences: prayer, the Word, fellowship, service.

And do not turn nostalgia for a past season into the goal. The purpose is not to feel exactly what you felt before, but to return to a living and obedient relationship with the Lord who is still speaking to his church.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
