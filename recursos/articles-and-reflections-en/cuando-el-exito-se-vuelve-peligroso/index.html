When success becomes dangerous

Base Text

"Beware that you do not forget the LORD your God." Deuteronomy 8:11

Israel needed to hear this warning not in the wilderness, but while thinking about good houses, food, and abundance.

Need can make us cry out; prosperity can make us forget. Moses warned that they would come to say: "My power and the might of my hand have gained me this wealth."

Success has a quiet way of rewriting our autobiography until we become the sole authors of everything good.

The Bible does not condemn the fruit of work. It condemns forgetting the Giver and using his gifts as fuel for pride.

If things are going well for you, give thanks. Enjoy with sobriety. Be generous. Remember who gave you ability, opportunities, people, and time. Also ask what purpose what you have received can serve.

Abundance is a different test from scarcity. Both reveal where the heart is.

Do not wait to lose something before recognizing that it was never truly yours.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
