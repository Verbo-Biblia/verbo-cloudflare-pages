When you are exhausted from serving

Base Text

"Come aside by yourselves to a deserted place and rest a while." Mark 6:31

Jesus said these words when the disciples had so much activity that they did not even have time to eat. It is significant that he did not praise their exhaustion as proof of spirituality. He called them to rest.

Scripture honors service, but it does not turn depletion into a virtue. We are creatures, not the Messiah. We need sleep, food, pauses, and the recognition of limits.

At the same time, biblical rest is not abandoning love for our neighbor. In that same account the crowd appeared and Jesus had compassion. There are needs that interrupt plans. That is why we need wisdom, not a rigid rule.

Sometimes serving requires sacrifice; at other times continuing without rest comes from the pride of believing that everything depends on us. Jesus could withdraw to pray even while needs remained around him.

If you are exhausted, examine your load. What truly belongs to you? What did you take on in order to prove something? What can you share with others? Rest can be a humble way of confessing that the work belongs to God and you are his servant, not his replacement.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
