When you do not know what decision to make

Base Text

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God." James 1:5

The Bible does not promise a supernatural sign for every decision. Many times we would like God to choose for us between two jobs, two cities, or two opportunities. James speaks of asking for wisdom in the midst of trials, an ability to live faithfully before God.

Biblical wisdom begins with the fear of the Lord and learns to distinguish what is good. So before looking for signs, ask what God has revealed. If one option requires sin, you already have an answer. If both are legitimate, you can examine responsibilities, motives, consequences, and wise counsel.

Paul made decisions, changed plans, and at times found doors closed. He did not live paralyzed while waiting for absolute certainty. There are decisions in which God allows us to exercise responsible freedom.

Pray, seek wisdom, listen to counsel, know your motives, and decide without turning every coincidence into an oracle. Your final security does not rest on having guessed a secret map, but on belonging to Christ as you walk.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
