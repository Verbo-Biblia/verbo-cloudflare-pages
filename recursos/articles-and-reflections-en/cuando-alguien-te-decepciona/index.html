When someone disappoints you

Base Text

"At my first defense no one stood with me... But the Lord stood with me." 2 Timothy 4:16–17

Paul knew what it was to be abandoned by people close to him. He did not need to pretend that it did not hurt: he plainly said that no one stood with him. Yet he did not end the sentence there. The Lord stood with him.

Sometimes we turn a person into the place where we expect to find a faithfulness that only God can sustain perfectly. When that person fails, everything seems to collapse.

The biblical response is not to stop trusting human beings. Paul continued to have coworkers and asked for Mark to come to him. Community matters. But no friendship, marriage, leadership, or family can bear the weight of being our savior.

We must also avoid using "God never fails" to deny wounds that need conversation, correction, or even prudent distance. Paul named harmful behavior. Grace does not require naivety.

If someone disappointed you, bring that wound to Christ. Then decide what love requires: forgive, speak, set boundaries, restore where possible. And remember that even when a human chair is left empty, the Lord does not abandon his own.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
