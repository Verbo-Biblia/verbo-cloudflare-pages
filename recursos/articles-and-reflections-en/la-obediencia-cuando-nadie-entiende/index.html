Obedience when no one understands

Base Text

"We ought to obey God rather than men." Acts 5:29

Peter spoke these words before authorities who ordered them to stop proclaiming Jesus.

This text is not permission to despise all authority or turn personal preferences into heroic acts of disobedience. The same apostles taught respect for authorities.

The limit appears when an authority demands something that clearly contradicts God. Then the higher loyalty belongs to the Lord.

That obedience can cost something. The apostles were beaten. They were not promised that defending a conviction would always produce admiration.

If you face pressure, first make sure your position truly comes from Scripture and not simply from pride or tradition.

Then speak respectfully, accept the consequences you cannot avoid, and remain faithful.

Christ is Lord even when obeying him is unpopular.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
