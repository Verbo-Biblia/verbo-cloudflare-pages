Gratitude on ordinary days

Base Text

"In everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you." 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Paul says "in everything," not "for everything."

Scripture does not require us to thank God for evil as though it were good. We can lament injustice, illness, and death.

However, no circumstance exhausts everything for which we can give thanks.

Christian gratitude comes from recognizing that our life is received, not manufactured. Food, people, opportunities, forgiveness, and ultimately Christ are gifts before they are rights.

Ingratitude makes the ordinary invisible because it notices only what is missing.

Practicing gratitude is not denying problems; it is preventing them from occupying the whole field of vision.

Today name concrete things before God, not generalities. Give thanks for a person, a provision, a truth of the gospel, a small mercy.

And if the day is dark, begin with what remains: in Christ we have a hope circumstances cannot cancel.

Prayer

Lord, bring this truth of your Word into my life. Give me wisdom to obey you sincerely, a humble heart, and eyes fixed on Jesus Christ. Amen.
